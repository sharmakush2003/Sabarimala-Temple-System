Divine Shrine & History

Established in 1990 (Reg No. 428/90), the Sabarimala Sri Ayyappaswamy Temple in Hubballi has stood as a sacred beacon of spiritual devotion, Hindu religious values, and charity. Dedicated to Lord Ayyappa and Sri Durga Devi, the shrine hosts daily poojas, weekly bhajan sandhyas, and serves as an important gathering point for pilgrims making the annual holy pilgrimage to the Sabarimala hill shrine in Kerala.

Under the dedicated guidance of the Bhajan Mandali, the temple performs spiritual services, conducts major festivals like Mandalapooja and Makara Jyothi, and fosters social welfare through Annadana (free food distribution) and charitable initiatives for the local community.

System Operating Manual & User Guide

Complete step-by-step instructions for temple administrative staff and counter operators.

1. How to Create & Print Invoices (Counter Billing)

Generating official paper donation vouchers step-by-step
STEP 1

Enter Devotee Details

Type the Devotee's Name (Sri / Smt) and their 10-digit Mobile Number. The mobile number helps in searching donation history in the registry later.

STEP 2

Select Seva / Pooja Purpose

Select from 25 predefined official temple Sevas (e.g. Mahapooja, Annadana, Nitya Seva, Archana). Choose "Custom Purpose" if entering a special custom donation amount.

STEP 3

Select Date & Payment Mode

Select payment mode: Cash, UPI (enter Transaction/Ref ID), or Cheque (enter Cheque No). Amount in Words is calculated automatically!

STEP 4

Language Toggle & Print

Check the Live Voucher Preview. Use English Preview or Kannada Preview buttons to select voucher language, then click "Generate & Print Receipt Voucher" to print.

2. Donation Registry & Management Dashboard

Searching, filtering, re-printing, and exporting donation data

Instant Live Search

Type any Devotee Name, Receipt Number (e.g. 004), Mobile Number, or Seva Name in the search box to find matching records instantly.

Payment Mode Filters

Click filter pills (All, Cash, UPI, Cheque) to isolate collection channels and view breakdown statistics for audit purposes.

Export to Excel / CSV

Click "Export CSV" to download complete donation registry files for Excel bookkeeping, auditing, or temple archives.

3. Quick Reference Tips for Operators

Important features for smooth counter operation
Re-printing Old Vouchers

Go to Donation Registry, locate the receipt, and click "Print Voucher". You can switch language to English or Kannada inside the modal before printing.

Safe Local Storage

All billing data is automatically saved in browser local storage. Data stays safe even if you refresh the browser page or restart the computer.
